📖 How to play

⛏️ Mine: Gain 2 gems.
🥷 Swipe: Steal 1 gem from a random miner. If nobody mines, a random guard blocks you and gains 1 gem; otherwise you get nothing.
🛡️ Guard: Protect yourself. Gain 1 gem if you block a swipe.
🏆 First to reach 10 gems wins! Moves stay secret until everyone locks in.
